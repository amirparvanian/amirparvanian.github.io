Project 01 — Senegal, Africa · 2024

Nene Yaye

Maternal care, a school and a community room in one building — off-grid, built from earth block and timber.

  • Senegal, Africa
  • 2024
  • Earth block & timber
1 / 2

Project workflow

Modelled and detailed in Revit, including the structural details of the roof. Construction drawings were exported from that model, and Enscape was used to study light during design, not to produce the final images.

Shows: site analysis, complex plan development, climate-driven section.

Technical dossier

ModellingRevit — full model including structural roof details; construction drawings exported from the model
Light studiesEnscape — used to study light during design, not for final images
StrategyPassive design · life-cycle assessment · earth block and timber, one repeated room module

Location selection

Fafa Kourou, in the Kolda region. The site was chosen for its climate and for its position on the main road between two larger towns — the point where the villages around it can reach care.

Project concept

The brief asked for maternal care, a school and a community room in one building, in a village with no reliable grid power. The rooms sit in a ring around a single courtyard, so every room gets outside air and shade. The public and private wings are separated by the courtyard instead of by a wall.

The plan

Rooms in a ring around one courtyard. The courtyard is the meeting point of the building, and the ring shades itself. The form refers to protection and care, and it does a job: every room faces the courtyard.

Sustainable approach

Built from earth block and timber, with one room module repeated across the plan, so local labour can build it. Deep overhangs and cross ventilation through the courtyard mean the building needs no mechanical cooling. The roof is the collection surface: rain runs to a tank that feeds the courtyard planting.

Project 02 — Master’s thesis · Politecnico di Torino

Eshratabad Palace

A four-storey Qajar pavilion of 1881, listed in 1967, standing inside an active military site without regular maintenance.

  • Tehran, Iran
  • 2026
  • Master’s thesis — Politecnico di Torino

Role: archival research, condition assessment and drawings.

Role: masterplan, all-level plans, lift core, reinforcement and façade strategy (1:1000, 1:75, 1:20).

Technical dossier

Scales1:1000 masterplan · 1:75 plans · 1:20 reinforcement and façade strategy
SurveyAll four levels drawn at survey scale from archival photos, maps and five case studies
MethodInvestigate first · repair before replacement · compatible, distinct, reversible

A four-storey Qajar pavilion, built in 1881 and listed on Iran’s heritage register in 1967, now stands inside an active military site, without regular maintenance.

With access limited to exterior views, I used archival photos, maps and five case studies to draw all four levels at survey scale. Visible damage includes eroded brick and mortar, lost ornament, stains and cracks. Water control comes first. The palace’s significance also lies in its garden, circular basin and lost pavilions.

No measurements, material tests or calibrated simulations were possible; this is a preliminary diagnosis.

Condition and proposal, room by room

A cultural research centre uses the existing rooms, movable fittings and discreet services. I propose a lift core in the steep stair’s shaft, with a narrow, locally steel-braced opening in the brick wall. The vaults, central hall and timber ceilings remain intact.

The garden centres on the circular basin, with detached studios marking the lost pavilion sites. Investigate first; repair before replacement. Keep new work compatible, distinct and reversible.

Project 03 — Alassio, Italy · Italia & Partners

Andora Hotel & Spa

A closed 1970s block that turned away from the sea, reopened to the water — facade, rooms and wellness interior.

  • Alassio, Italy
  • 2026
  • Italia & Partners

The hotel

My role: facade development, room planning, sections, visualisation.

Shows: facade development, plan and section production, hotel planning.

Technical dossier

FacadePackage: balcony types · glazing module · 1:20 details
PlansRoom layouts 1:50 · spa 1:50 plan separating wet and dry, every drain located · pool edge detail
SchedulesFinishes listed by room with a reason for each · lighting keyed to scenes

A closed 1970s block that turned away from the sea: heavy concrete balconies, small openings.

  • Continuous balconies follow the coastline, replacing the solid facade.
  • Floor-to-ceiling glazing removes the barrier to the water.
  • The roof becomes a lounge, giving the view back.

The balcony is where the guest meets the sea. Its depth and shading decide the room.

What I produced: facade package — balcony types, glazing module, 1:20 details | room layouts at 1:50 | floor plans and sections | visualisation.

The spa

My role: interior layout, finishes and lighting schedules, wet-area detailing.

Shows: interior documentation, finishes and lighting schedules, material specification, wet-area detailing.

Reception, changing rooms, pool, sauna and hammam, then treatment: each space quieter and darker than the last.

  • The 1:50 plan separates wet and dry and locates every drain.
  • The finishes schedule lists materials by room, with a reason for each.
  • Lighting keyed to scenes — pool, sauna, treatment — so a room changes character without changing the ceiling.
  • The pool edge detail resolves drainage and waterproofing in one drawing.

Marble floors take the water. Dark slatted wood stays warm against stone. Glass encloses the wellness rooms without closing them.

Project 05 — Rome, Italy · Private client

Roma Master Suite

One quiet room: a soft palette, no strong colour, nothing that fights for attention.

  • Rome, Italy
  • 2026
  • Private client

My role: interior design, joinery drawings, finishes and lighting schedules, visualisation.

Shows: interior design development, joinery detailing, finishes and lighting schedules.

Modelled in 3ds Max, rendered in D5.

Technical dossier

Joinery1:20 drawings for the slatted wall and the bed unit
DrawingsInterior plans and elevations · finishes and fittings schedule by room
LightingLayout with separate circuits — wall lights and mirror backlight

The client asked for one quiet room: a soft palette, no strong colour, nothing that fights for attention.

  • A full-height slatted timber wall sits behind the bed with the lighting built into it. It hides the en-suite door and the wardrobe, and gives the room one continuous line.
  • A black-framed glass door separates the bedroom from the en-suite, so the two rooms read as one space while the wall stays closed.
  • The en-suite is beige plaster with brass fittings and a shaped backlit mirror.
  • Wall lights and the mirror backlight sit on separate circuits, so the evening scene is not just the daytime scene turned down.

What I produced: interior plans and elevations | joinery drawings at 1:20 for the slatted wall and the bed unit | finishes and fittings schedule by room | lighting layout with separate circuits.

Project 07 — Noli, Liguria

Palumbo Noli

A small home where the terrace counts as a room, not as decoration.

  • Noli, Liguria
  • 2026
  • Apartment and terrace

My role: interior design, terrace design, joinery and lighting drawings.

Shows: residential interiors, small-space planning, exterior detailing and lighting.

Interior modelled in 3ds Max, visualised in Enscape, including the terrace at dusk.

Technical dossier

DrawingsApartment plan 1:50 · interior elevations
DetailsArched wall and terrace deck details
OutsidePlanting and lighting layout detailed together — terrace usable after dark

Half of this apartment is outside. The client wanted a small home where the terrace counts as a room, not as decoration.

Inside:
  • Herringbone timber floors and curved furniture let the rooms run into each other without doors.
  • A custom arched wall around the television keeps the wall clean and puts the storage behind it.
  • Wall lighting instead of a ceiling fixture, placed so no lamp is visible from the seating.
Outside:
  • The terrace works as a second living room: timber decking, a planted wall and warm string lighting for the evening.
  • Planting and lighting were detailed together, so the terrace is usable after dark.

What I produced: apartment plan at 1:50 | interior elevations | details for the arched wall and the terrace deck | planting and lighting layout.
